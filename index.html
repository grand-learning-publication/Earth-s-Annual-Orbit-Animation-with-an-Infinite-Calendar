Calendar Engine
Follows the supplied historical registry rules for BCE/CE year length, leap years, month lengths, weekdays, and the 1582 calendar transition. Season labels follow the displayed equinox/solstice cycle.
Date Calculation
Uses integer calendar arithmetic rather than the browser Date object, so Day of Year and 365/366 remain stable for BCE and large years.
Sun F₁
The Sun is drawn at F₁, the first focus of the displayed ellipse. This focal geometry is intentionally exaggerated for visibility.
Empty Focus F₂
F₂ is the second focus of the displayed ellipse and contains no astronomical body. Its marker remains fixed to the ellipse geometry.
Earth Axial Tilt
Earth is shown with a 23.44° axial tilt. The tilted red axis remains attached to Earth while the surface rotates.
Visual Model
Ellipse eccentricity is exaggerated for visibility. Earth, F₁, F₂, axial tilt and event positions stay anchored to the displayed ellipse, while Northern and Southern Hemisphere seasons are shown as opposite seasonal states.
